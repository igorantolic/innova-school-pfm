Interview 3 – General Feedback
Positive aspects:
Card products are well regarded.
Mobile banking application is positively perceived.
No major operational issues were reported.
Areas for improvement:
Clearer and more proactive notifications.
Better communication regarding available products and functionalities.
Enhanced customer guidance, particularly for newly onboarded or migrated clients.
Summary:Overall, the customer experience appears positive, especially regarding digital banking services and day-to-day products. However, there is room for improvement in customer communication, product awareness, and the onboarding experience during major system changes.
Overall Conclusion
The interviews indicate generally strong customer satisfaction with the bank's digital capabilities, particularly the mobile application, card products, and ATM network. The most recurring concerns relate to communication during migration processes, transparency of information, and customer awareness of available services. At the same time, the feedback suggests potential opportunities to strengthen engagement through investment and insurance offerings while continuing to improve the overall customer journey.
